# Good Crowd Inc — Website Inspiration References

This document records the approved inspiration references for the Good Crowd website. These are behavioural and compositional references only. Do not copy visual identities, proprietary assets, source code, typography systems, or content from these sites.

## Existing references

### The Conference Group
https://theconferencegroup.com.au/not-for-profit/

Use for:
- contemporary not-for-profit presentation;
- editorial hierarchy and generous spacing;
- strong mission-led section progression.

### Event Society
https://eventsociety.com.au/our-team/

Use for:
- animated/scrolled number presentation;
- editorial pacing and personality.

Good Crowd constraint: never fabricate metrics. Only measured outcomes may be presented as achieved statistics.

### Interchange WA
https://interchangewa.org.au/

Use for:
- moving/layered cards inspired by “Our Approach”;
- tile colour transitions on hover/focus;
- tactile responsive interactions.

Primary Good Crowd application: People / Place / Prosperity and Values.

## Extended Awwwards references

### Ascend
https://ascendmarketing.xyz/

Use for:
- one continuous visual journey down the page;
- scroll-linked image/graphic movement;
- a secondary vertical logo/client rail on wide desktop.

Good Crowd translation:
- create a separate infinity-derived SVG path that visually originates near the hero/brand lockup and travels through the homepage as scroll progresses;
- do not deform or animate the supplied production logo itself;
- allow the infinity path to pass behind/through sections and resolve near the footer/team area;
- partner/community logos may use a vertical rail only when real approved logos exist;
- on tablet/mobile replace the rail with a horizontal or static responsive treatment.

### Jaéco
https://www.jaeco.fr/

Use for:
- cinematic project/event video;
- editorial case-study flow;
- project stories that combine narrative with prominent outcome metrics.

Good Crowd translation:
- introduce a reusable Event Story / Impact Story component architecture;
- support poster image, muted inline video, region/date, editorial story, measured metrics, gallery/media, and acknowledgements;
- animated figures should reveal measured outcomes only;
- Business Plan targets, projections, or placeholders must never appear as achieved results.

### Verticalloop
https://www.verticalloop.com/

Use for:
- purpose-led overall visual restraint;
- oversized editorial typography;
- strong whitespace;
- integrated scrolling/client-logo presentation rather than boxed corporate sections.

Good Crowd translation:
- retain warmth and restraint even when adding motion;
- avoid over-designing every section;
- let photography, typography, and the infinity journey carry the experience.

### Infinity Constellation
https://www.infinityconstellation.com/

Use for:
- connected, portrait-led founder/team presentation;
- people presented as part of a wider system rather than generic profile cards.

Good Crowd translation:
- use portrait-led founder/team treatment with oval/circular crops and restrained infinity geometry;
- profiles may gently come forward on hover/focus while retaining keyboard and touch accessibility;
- mobile should become a clear stacked editorial profile layout.

## Signature homepage motion hierarchy

The homepage must have one dominant interaction language rather than multiple competing effects.

1. Primary: infinity-derived scroll journey.
2. Secondary: People / Place / Prosperity and Values card motion.
3. Tertiary: event/video transitions and measured number reveals.
4. Supporting: partner/community logo movement.
5. Micro-interactions: buttons, links, hover/focus colour changes.

Avoid scroll-jacking, excessive parallax, cursor hijacking, or motion that delays content access.

All motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and preserve equivalent content/functionality without animation.
