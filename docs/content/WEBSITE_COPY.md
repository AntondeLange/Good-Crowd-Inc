## About

The About page should communicate the approved proposition that Good Crowd Inc is a not-for-profit operating on a simple but powerful belief: when communities collaborate, everyone wins.

Explain that Good Crowd exists to connect regional communities, celebrate what makes them unique and unlock economic and social potential through connection and high-quality events.

The organisation does not work within community boundaries in isolation; the model is about bringing neighbouring regions together to create something none of them could achieve alone.

### Purpose and vision
Use the approved source material for purpose, vision and not-for-profit commitment. Do not embellish with unsupported claims.
